A collection of essays on natural waters and ecosystems, practical craft and building, the life of towns and families, and the Western intellectual tradition.

Writings are indexed across four persistent coordinates: 
+ [*physis*](/coordinates/physis/) (the physical world, nature, and water)
+ [*technē*](/coordinates/techne/) (tools, craft, and engineering)
+ [*polis*](/coordinates/polis/) (community and institutions)
+ [*logos*](/coordinates/logos/) (philosophy, literature, and theology)
